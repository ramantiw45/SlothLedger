# Surface brief — Sloth Ledger, ledger page

**Mode:** Operate. **Scope:** the single page that logs chores, judges them, and totals reclaimed time.

## The job

The builder finishes a session and wants to know what their automations actually saved. They type a chore from memory, get a verdict, run the automation, and leave with one number they can defend to a stranger.

## Constraints

Runs offline from `python app.py`; no webfonts, no CDN, no build step. Keyboard complete, AA contrast, 44px targets, reduced motion honoured. Demo data is labelled; no invented savings.

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** The ledger is a library date-stamp tray, not a dashboard. Chores are typed cards; verdicts are rubber stamps; reclaiming a chore punches its card; totals are restamped. It refuses the KPI-tile grid, the hero-metric card row, and the gradient SaaS shell.

**OWN-WORLD:** Dark desk felt (#14201B) under manila cards (#E7DCBE). Ink #1B1A17, library violet #4A3F7A for delegate, vermilion #B33A24 for automate-refused errors, ruled blue (#8FA6B8) column rules. Type: condensed grotesque for stamps and headings, mono for everything typed on a card. No radii beyond 2px, no drop shadows — cards sit by edge and ink, not by glow.

**STORY:** "Every line shows its arithmetic, and the total is stamped from those lines." The visitor reads the evidence column before the number, then submits their own chore and watches a card land in the tray.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** Left: a TOTALS card with the week's reclaimed time stamped large, double-ruled, and three smaller stamped lines beneath (all time, still on you, chores judged). Right: the entry card — a blank index card with a dotted typing line, an optional minutes box, and one solid STAMP button. The first tray card sits below, evidence line visible in mono.

**FORM:** Assigned candidate 6 of 7 grounded worlds (library date-stamp card tray). Seed key `a3371c1d`. Alternates carried: columnar audit book (top-ranked, shown as the pick).

**FINISH:** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
